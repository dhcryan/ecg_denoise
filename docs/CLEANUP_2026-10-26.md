- Participant recordings, real-data weights and outputs: `ajou_data*`, `ajou_real`, `ajou_finetune`, `ajou_outputs*`, `kayoung`, and `solmi`.
- Real-data reports and delivery packages: `reports/`, `deliverables/`, the interim generalization report, and `docs/history/`.
- Archives and cleanup backups, except the public `archive/README.md`.
- Five real-data notebooks, regardless of whether they are at the old root location or under `notebooks/`: `ajou_ecg_deepfilter_beataug.ipynb`, `wet_model_compare_report.ipynb`, `finetuned.ipynb`, `finetuned_improved.ipynb`, and `ecg_processing_ref.ipynb`.
- Credential files, machine configuration, runtime caches, and generated binary artifacts.

Five already-tracked private paths were removed from Git's index with `git rm --cached`: the legacy Ajou, fine-tuning, improved fine-tuning, and ECG reference notebook paths, plus `docs/INTERIM_GENERALIZATION_REPORT_260101.md`. The notebooks remain at their organized local paths, and the interim report remains on disk. These index removals are staged; no commit or push was made.

A real participant name in a source-code example was replaced with `Subject01`. Six public notebooks now use repository-relative paths in place of machine-specific paths, and personal home directories were redacted from textual outputs. Numeric results, images, cell order, and execution counts were retained. Machine-specific Conda `prefix` lines were removed from all three environment specifications. Original authorship and attribution headers were preserved.

Ignore rules and index removal do **not** erase earlier commits or remote copies. Existing Git history was not rewritten. Future outputs from local real-data workflows remain covered by the directory and notebook rules.
